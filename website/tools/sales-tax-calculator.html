Adding Tax vs Stripping Tax Formulas

  • Tax Inclusive (Adding Tax): $\text{Tax} = \text{Net} \times (R / 100)$, $\text{Gross} = \text{Net} + \text{Tax}$
  • Tax Exclusive (Extracting Inclusive Tax): $\text{Net} = \text{Gross} / (1 + R / 100)$, $\text{Tax} = \text{Gross} - \text{Net}$

The Critical Arithmetic Difference Between Adding and Removing Tax

Adding tax involves direct multiplication. Removing tax is where bookkeepers and small business owners frequently commit accounting errors. You cannot subtract the tax percentage from a gross figure: if you take 20% off $120, you subtract $24, arriving at $96—which is incorrect. The true net base is $100 ($120 / 1.20) and the tax collected was $20.
